KAGARIS

ANCIENT RED DRAGON

Kagaris is an ancient dragon that awakened in the primordial flames long before the dawn of human history.

In that primeval age, when rivers of lava carved across the continent and volcanic ash darkened the skies, it forged a pact with the ancient race known as the Eldar and stood as guardian of their lands.

Its mountain-like body and claws honed like spearheads strike terror into all who face it, while its crimson scales, tempered by the heat of volcanoes over countless ages, are harder than steel armor.

When it opens its massive maw, the glow of a blazing furnace flickers deep within its throat, and the flames that pour forth soon sweep across the land, reducing everything in their path to ash.

VOLGRAS

Once an ordinary wolf, the Volgras is an evolved species that underwent a monstrous mutation after consuming Blackstone, a remnant of Eldar.

The mutation caused its upper body and forelimbs to develop abnormally, resulting in a massive physique and a distinctive form of movement known as knuckle-walking.

Although extremely rare, Volgras are known to command packs of ordinary wolves. They primarily inhabit deep forests, where they hunt alongside the wolf packs under their control.

LITHIVORE

The Lithivore is a mineral-feeding beast that primarily inhabits mountainous regions and mineral-rich mines.

The first mineral consumed after birth determines its lifelong diet. Over time, the minerals it ingests accumulate within its body and gradually emerge through its skin, forming a hardened layer of natural armor.

Its appearance and physical characteristics vary depending on the minerals it consumes, giving rise to several distinct subspecies, including Crystallbores, Opalbores, and Diabores.
