Character

Sellsword

KIAN

◇

Driven purely by the promise of a payout, he's a man who will do whatever it takes to get the job done.

But a request from the House of Arkin entangles him in a massive destiny.

You won't find any elegant finesse or chivalry in staying alive and going straight for the enemy's throat.

Princess of the Nillen

ELIA

◇

Burdened by the weight of the Arkin name, she is a non-combatant who lacks the strength to even defend herself.

Despite this, her good heart makes it impossible for her to turn a blind eye to the tragedies of the weak.

Her blind altruism sometimes drags her companions into unpredictable dangers.

However, even in the face of a cruel fate that constantly pushes her to the brink, showing kindness to others is her own unique way of fighting to preserve her dignity.

The Elf warrior

RIAN

◇

Fortified by a noble sense of duty, she stands resolute amidst the shallow desires of mortals and blood-soaked chaos.

Bound by her mission to protect one the Destined, she eradicates any threat that bars their path.

Her elegant yet formidable power, honed over countless years, serves as an unshakable barrier for the party.

Dwarven boy

VIGO

◇

Despite being a dwarf, he can't control his immense strength and is unable to forge the proud weapons of his people.

Built only to destroy and crush instead of crafting iron, he grew up feeling deeply inadequate and alienated.

However, beneath his destructive abilities lies a tender heart.

With unshakable perseverance, he relentlessly pursues his dream of opening his own forge.
